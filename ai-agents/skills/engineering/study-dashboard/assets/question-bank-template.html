Foundational general and ads system design questions, plus the ones most often reported for big ads companies. Company questions link back to the foundational pattern they test. All study dashboards

How to use this bank

Most company questions are variations of about 25 foundational patterns. Master the patterns first, then practice each company's twist. Answer out loud with a timer (35 to 45 minutes) before opening a card.

Answer framework (use for every question)

1. ClarifyUsers, scope, functional requirements, what is out of scope.
2. TensionName the two requirements that fight (for example latency vs exactness).
3. EstimateQPS, storage, bandwidth; only the numbers that change the design.
4. API and dataCore endpoints, entities, keys, partitioning.
5. ArchitectureEnd-to-end happy path first, then scale each hot spot.
6. Deep diveFailure modes, consistency, hot keys, cost, monitoring.
