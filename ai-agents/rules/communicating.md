# Communicating rules

Covers docs, readouts, status updates, messages, and review comments written for people.

## Guardrails

- Never overstate certainty; mark what is verified, inferred, and unknown.
- Never include secrets, private data, or internal-only detail in content meant for a wider audience.

## Preferences

- Lead with the conclusion or ask, then the supporting detail.
- Match depth and vocabulary to the audience; cut anything the reader does not need.
- State risk and confidence plainly instead of hedging.
